## Get started

1. Download **[TarkaSetup.exe](https://github.com/medha-labs/tarka/releases/latest/download/TarkaSetup.exe)** and open it. No admin rights or other software needed.
2. Windows shows **Windows protected your PC** because the installer isn't code-signed. Choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
3. Tarka opens on its own. *Set up Tarka* asks your trading style and watchlist, then downloads market data. Open **Funds** once and Tarka builds 5 years of fund history (about 10 to 15 minutes).

Tarka checks for updates when it starts and offers them; your settings, lists and history are kept. The **[user guide](https://github.com/medha-labs/tarka/releases/latest/download/Tarka-User-Manual.pdf)** covers every screen, the morning Brief and the FAQ.

<details>
<summary><b>AI analysis (optional)</b></summary>

Choose the engine in Settings, AI:

| Engine | What you need | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Gemini** | A key from [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com) | Free tier |
| **OpenAI** | A key from [platform.openai.com](https://platform.openai.com) | Pay per use |
| **Claude API** | A key from [console.anthropic.com](https://console.anthropic.com) | Pay per use |
| **Claude Code** | Your own Claude login | Your Claude plan |

Keys stay in Windows Credential Manager on your PC. A ChatGPT Plus or Gemini app subscription is not an API key. The AI only describes what Tarka's rules found and never gives buy or sell advice.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Your data and privacy</b></summary>

Everything lives on your PC (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tarka`). Tarka has no account and no server of its own. It fetches public market data, and, only if you turn them on, sends a stock's details to the AI engine you chose and the morning Brief through your own Gmail. Uninstalling keeps your data; delete that folder to remove it.

</details>
